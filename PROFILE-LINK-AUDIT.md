## Search and AI discovery notes
Google ignores the HTML meta keywords tag. Clear language pages, helpful content, verified business profiles, accessible crawl paths, JSON-LD that matches visible content and a sitemap help discovery, not guaranteed ranking. A text file like llms.txt does not confer priority in Google AI results.
